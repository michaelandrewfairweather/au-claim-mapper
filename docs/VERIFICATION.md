## Findings worth noting

- **Vic previously settled child abuse claims** (s 27OA): only settlements before 1 July 2018, or between 13 November 2024 and the commencement of the Justice Legislation Amendment (Vicarious Liability for Child Abuse) Act 2026, count. National Redress Scheme deeds are excluded (s 27QA(3)).
- **The WA employer's legal structure decides the system**: a small WA partnership's employee may have 28 days at the WAIRC, or 21 days at the FWC, and may not know which. The tool shows both and uses the earlier date.
- **AHRC court costs protection (s 46PSA)** is strong but not absolute: a successful respondent can get costs where it has no significant power or resources advantage over the applicant.
